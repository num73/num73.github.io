About Him

He is a Ph.D. student at ShanghaiTech University, majoring in Computer Science, and his advisor is Prof. Shu Yin. Prior to that, he obtained his bachelor's degree from Zhengzhou University. After graduating, he worked as a software engineer for about one year, and then entered ShanghaiTech University to continue his studies.

His research interest is in storage systems, especially those related to operating systems.

He is now working on things about persistent memory.
